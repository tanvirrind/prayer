Located in the heart of Dubai, UAE, Al Twar Second is a rapidly growing residential and commercial area that plays host to a diverse population of Muslim residents. The area is a significant hub for commerce, entertainment, and community life, with a multitude of shops, restaurants, and businesses catering to the needs of its residents. As a predominantly Muslim community, prayer times are an integral part of daily life, and residents can access various mosques to perform their five daily prayers.

The Muslim community in Al Twar Second is known for its strong sense of Islamic values and tradition. Many residents frequent the local mosques for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha prayers, which take place according to a precise schedule that is calculated based on the lunar calendar. Islamic culture is deeply embedded in the community, with many residents observing the holy month of Ramadan and participating in other Islamic events and celebrations throughout the year. The area's numerous mosques, community centers, and Islamic institutions provide a platform for Muslims to gather, worship, and engage in charitable activities.

Al Twar Second experiences a hot desert climate throughout the year, with extremely high temperatures during the summer months and mild winters. Residents and visitors should plan their trip accordingly and prepare for the heat during the summer. The best time to visit the area is from October to March, when the weather is cooler and more conducive for outdoor activities. Understanding the local climate and prayer times can help make your visit to Al Twar Second a more enjoyable and spiritually uplifting experience.
