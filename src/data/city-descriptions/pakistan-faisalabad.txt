Located in the Punjab province of Pakistan, Faisalabad is the country's third-largest city and a major industrial hub. Its strategic location has made it a significant city, boasting a population of over 3.2 million residents. Faisalabad has also been named one of Pakistan's fastest-growing cities, attracting businesses and entrepreneurs from across the nation. For Muslims, Faisalabad serves as an important center for Islamic life, with numerous mosques throughout the city, providing a hub for prayer times and community gatherings.

The city's large Muslim community is deeply rooted in Islamic traditions and practices. Islamic festivals and events are celebrated with great enthusiasm, and the city's residents come together to perform prayers during special occasions. Faisalabad's Muslim population actively participates in communal prayers, ensuring the city remains a strong symbol of community and faith. With numerous Islamic schools, colleges, and centers, Faisalabad has a strong foundation for Islamic learning and education, catering to residents and visitors alike.

Faisalabad has a hot desert climate, characterized by extreme temperature fluctuations. The best times to visit the city are during the cooler months (October to February), when temperatures range from 10°C to 25°C. For those planning to visit during these months, it is essential to check the city's prayer times, which can be easily accessed through online resources and mobile applications. By taking advantage of the mild weather and convenient access to Islamic resources, visitors can plan their trips accordingly and enjoy Faisalabad's unique blend of culture and faith.
