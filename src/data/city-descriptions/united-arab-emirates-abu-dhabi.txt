Located on the southeast coast of the Arabian Gulf, Abu Dhabi is the capital city of the United Arab Emirates. As the largest and most populous city in the UAE, it boasts a significant presence of Muslims and Islamic institutions, offering a unique blend of modernization and traditional Islamic values. With a rich Islamic heritage and history, Abu Dhabi is an important hub for Muslim community and cultural exchange.

Home to the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, one of the largest mosques in the world, Abu Dhabi is a beacon for Islamic faith and learning. The city's impressive collection of mosques, cultural centers, and Islamic museums reflects its commitment to preserving and promoting Islamic culture. As a predominantly Muslim city, Abu Dhabi observes prayer times and Islamic traditions with great reverence and fervor.

Abu Dhabi experiences a hot desert climate with very little rainfall throughout the year, making the best time to visit between November and March when temperatures are more bearable. Visitors can attend prayers at various mosques, including the iconic Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, which holds prayer sessions five times a day. When planning your trip, check the prayer times for Abu Dhabi and join the local prayers to experience the city's strong devotion to Islam firsthand.
