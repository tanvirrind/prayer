Located in the heart of the Chhattisgarh state of India, Abhanpur is a significant urban agglomeration with a population of over 70,000 people. This urban center is known for its rich cultural heritage, which reflects the diverse influences of its surrounding regions. For the Muslim community, Abhanpur holds immense importance, serving as a spiritual hub where they can practice their faith in a thriving environment. Muslims make up a considerable portion of the city's population, with several mosques and Islamic centers playing a vital role in their spiritual lives, ensuring they can stay connected with their faith through prayer times and other religious activities.

Islamic culture and traditions are woven deeply into the fabric of Abhanpur's society, with the city's Muslim community actively participating in various religious events and functions throughout the year. As Muslims navigate their faith, prayer times hold great significance, with mosques and Islamic centers providing a dedicated space for them to come together, pray, and engage in Islamic activities. This inclusive atmosphere fosters strong bonds among community members and promotes a deeper understanding of their faith.

Abhanpur experiences a humid subtropical climate, characterized by hot summers and mild winters. The best time to visit the city coincides with the winter months (October to March), when the weather is relatively pleasant and temperatures range from 12 to 25 degrees Celsius. Planning a trip during these periods can allow visitors to explore the city's attractions without the scorching heat, making it an ideal time to explore the rich cultural heritage of Abhanpur, including its vibrant Muslim community and prayer times.
