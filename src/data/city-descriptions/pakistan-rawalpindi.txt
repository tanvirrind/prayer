Located in the northwestern region of Pakistan, Rawalpindi is a significant city with a rich history and cultural heritage, serving as a gateway to the famous Hunza Valley and home to the country's largest ammunition depot. The city boasts a diverse population of over 3 million people and is an essential hub for trade, commerce, and transportation. As a predominantly Muslim city, Rawalpindi is steeped in Islamic traditions and values, with a significant Muslim population making up a substantial portion of its residents.

The city is dotted with numerous mosques and Islamic centers that cater to the spiritual needs of its Muslim community. From the historic and iconic Faisaliya Masjid to the more modern and well-equipped mosques, Rawalpindi's mosques serve as beacons of Islamic culture and faith, providing a sense of belonging and community to its Muslim residents and visitors alike. As prayer times are essential to the Muslim community, Rawalpindi's residents and visitors can find reliable and accurate prayer times with just a glance, helping them to plan their daily activities and worship according to Islamic teachings.

Rawalpindi's climate is subtropical, characterized by hot summers and mild winters, with average temperatures ranging from 32°C to 22°C throughout the year. While the best time to visit Rawalpindi is during the spring and autumn seasons (March to May and September to November), Muslims can plan their visits around significant Islamic festivals and holidays like Eid-al-Fitr and Eid-al-Adha, when prayer times and celebrations are at their peak. By visiting during these times, Muslims can experience the city's warm hospitality and rich Islamic heritage firsthand.
