Located in the southwestern region of Saudi Arabia, Abha is a significant city in the Asir Province, known for its rich history and stunning natural scenery. Abha has a population of approximately 1.5 million residents, with a substantial Muslim community forming the backbone of the city's culture and daily life. As Islam is the predominant faith in Abha, mosques play a vital role in the spiritual lives of its residents, offering residents five times a day prayer services, ensuring they stay connected to their faith.

Abha is renowned for its Islamic culture, which is deeply ingrained in the daily lives of its inhabitants. Muslim residents here live in harmony with their fellow citizens, embracing Islamic values and principles. This creates a tranquil environment, allowing individuals to focus on their spiritual growth and devotion. The numerous mosques throughout Abha cater to diverse needs of its Muslim community, providing an inclusive space for prayer services as well as community gatherings and educational events.

Abha enjoys a unique climate, characterized by low humidity and mild temperatures, particularly during the summer months, making it an ideal destination for travelers seeking refuge from the scorching heat. While visitors can explore the city's natural beauty at any time, it is advisable to visit Abha during the spring or autumn seasons, when temperatures are more pleasant and comfortable.
