Located in the heart of the Arabian Peninsula, Riyadh serves as the capital and largest city of Saudi Arabia, boasting a population of over six million residents. Its significance extends beyond its status as a major commercial and economic hub, with the city being a beacon for Muslim pilgrims and a center of Islamic learning. With a substantial Muslim population, Riyadh is home to numerous grand mosques, such as the Al-Fatih Mosque, which is one of the largest mosques in the world and a major landmark in the city.

As Islam plays a significant role in everyday life in Riyadh, Muslims living in the city often observe a strict adherence to Islamic principles and customs. The city offers a range of Islamic services and facilities, including mosques, Islamic schools, and community centers. Muslims in Riyadh also observe the traditional five daily prayers, which are usually performed in local mosques or prayer halls. The Muslim community in Riyadh is a melting pot of cultures, with people from various countries contributing to the city's rich tapestry of Islamic practices and traditions.

Situated in a hot desert climate, Riyadh experiences extremely high temperatures during the summer months. Therefore, the best time to visit the city is during the cooler winter months, from December to February, when comfortable temperatures range from 64°F to 82°F (18°C to 28°C). For Muslims living in or visiting Riyadh, it is essential to be aware of the prayer times and schedules to ensure timely observance of the five daily prayers.
