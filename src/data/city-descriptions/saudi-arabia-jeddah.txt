Located on the west coast of the Arabian Peninsula, Jeddah is a significant port city and a key entry point to Saudi Arabia. With a large population of around 4 million residents, including a thriving Muslim community, the city is known for its rich Islamic heritage and rich history dating back to the 7th century. Jeddah is a major hub for Islamic trade and commerce, attracting pilgrims and travelers from around the world.

The Muslim community in Jeddah plays a significant role in shaping the city's culture and identity, with numerous mosques and Islamic centers throughout the city, providing a platform for prayer, education and community service. Visitors can experience the city's unique blend of Islamic traditions and Arabic hospitality during their stay, making it an essential stop on any Muslim pilgrimage or travel itinerary. As one of the holiest cities in Islam, Muslims from all over the world come to Jeddah to pray and perform rituals.

Jeddah boasts a subtropical desert climate with very hot summers and mild winters, making the months from September to November and February to March the best time to visit. During these periods, the weather is mild and pleasant, ideal for outdoor activities, sightseeing, and attending mosque prayers, which can be easily found through prayer times online platforms. With its unique history and rich Islamic culture, Jeddah is a must-visit destination for any Muslim traveler.
