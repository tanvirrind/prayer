Located in the North-West Frontier Province of Pakistan, Bannu is a significant city in the region with a population of approximately 240,000 people. As a major urban center, Bannu serves as the administrative hub of Bannu District and is an important stopover on the Peshawar-Lahore railway line. The city is strategically situated at the foot of the Toba Achakzai mountain range, making it a significant economic and geographic center in the region.

Bannu boasts a rich Muslim heritage and culture, with the majority of its residents identifying as Muslim. The city is home to numerous mosques, shrines, and Islamic monuments, a testament to its long history of Islamic influence. The strong presence of Muslim communities here makes prayer times a crucial part of daily life, and locals can be seen observing their five daily prayers at various mosques throughout the city. Islamic culture plays a significant role in shaping the social and spiritual fabric of life in Bannu.

Bannu experiences a subtropical climate, characterized by hot summers and mild winters. The city receives significant rainfall during the monsoon season, resulting in pleasant temperatures during the winter months, making it an ideal time to visit for those interested in exploring its Islamic heritage. Whether it's the bustling bazaars or the historic mosques, Bannu is a city that offers a unique cultural experience, especially during the prayer times and Islamic events that fill its streets with faith and community spirit.
