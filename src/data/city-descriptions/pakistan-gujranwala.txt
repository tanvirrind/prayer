Located in the province of Punjab, Pakistan, Gujranwala is a prominent industrial city with a rich history and cultural significance. As the 11th most populous city in the country, it boasts an urban population of over 2.7 million inhabitants, with a notable presence of Pakistani Muslims who call Gujranwala their home. This thriving metropolis is renowned for its textile industry, which contributes significantly to the national economy, drawing inspiration from the rich Islamic heritage of the region.

The Muslim community in Gujranwala is deeply rooted in the city's fabric, with numerous mosques and Islamic centers serving as beacons of faith and guidance for its residents. The city is a testament to the enduring spirit of Pakistani Muslims, showcasing their love for Islam through vibrant cultural events and daily rituals. Prayer times, an essential aspect of the Islamic faith, are a vital part of Gujranwala's daily life, with Muslims gathering at mosques and open spaces to perform their five daily prayers in accordance with the call of the azan.

Gujranwala experiences a semi-arid climate, characterized by hot summers and mild winters, with an average annual rainfall of around 300 mm. The best time to visit Gujranwala is during the winter months, from December to February, when the weather is relatively pleasant, making it an ideal time for sightseeing and exploring the city's cultural attractions. Muslims visiting Gujranwala during this time can easily find prayer facilities and attend Islamic events, further enhancing their spiritual journey in the city.
