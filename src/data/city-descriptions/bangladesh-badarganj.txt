Located in the northern region of Bangladesh, Badarganj is a charming town situated in the Rangpur Division. With a population of over 43,000 residents, this city boasts a rich history and cultural significance. As a major center for trade and commerce, Badarganj draws visitors from across the country, who come to experience its ancient charm and strong sense of community.

Badarganj is home to a predominantly Muslim population, with a rich Islamic heritage that permeates every aspect of life. The town is dotted with numerous mosques, from grand, historic structures to simple, community-driven places of worship. Islamic culture is deeply ingrained in the daily lives of the residents, with prayer times a central aspect of routine. Visitors can experience firsthand the devotion and camaraderie of the Muslim community, where prayers and worship are a cornerstone of community life.

Badarganj has a subtropical climate, characterized by warm summers and mild winters. The best time to visit is from October to March, when temperatures range from 18-25°C (64-77°F), making it ideal for exploring the town's historic sites, markets, and cultural attractions. During this period, Muslims can enjoy optimal conditions for prayers and daily worship, with clear sunshine and pleasant weather.
