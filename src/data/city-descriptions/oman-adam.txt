Located in the southern governorate of Dhofar, Adam is a significant city in Oman, with a population of around 15,000 residents. The city serves as an important cultural and economic hub in the region, offering a glimpse into the rich heritage of the country. Adam is a popular destination for tourists drawn to its stunning landscapes, rich history, and unique cultural attractions.

The Muslim community in Adam plays a vital role in the city's fabric, with several mosques serving as central hubs for spiritual practices and community events. In Adam, Muslims live in harmony with the local population, celebrating Islamic festivals and adhering to their faith traditions. With an increasing number of Muslim residents and visitors, finding accurate prayer times has become easier, thanks to online resources and mobile apps. These tools help individuals stay informed about Salat times, allowing them to plan their day and participate in prayers as scheduled.

As for climate, Adam enjoys a tropical savanna climate year-round, with mild winters and hot, humid summers. The best time to visit Adam is during the cooler months between October and March, when temperatures range from 20-25°C. During this time, the weather is pleasant for outdoor activities, allowing visitors to explore the city's natural scenery and historical landmarks while staying comfortable.
