Located in the Eastern Province of Saudi Arabia, Abqaiq is a significant industrial city due to its oil refineries and petrochemical plants. With a population of approximately 120,000 residents, Abqaiq serves as a hub for businesses and families seeking opportunities in the region. The city's strategic position and diverse economic landscape make it an important center for growth.

As a predominantly Muslim city, Abqaiq is home to numerous mosques that provide a central gathering place for the community to perform their five daily prayers. The city's Islamic culture is deeply rooted, with a strong sense of tradition and community values. Abqaiq's Muslim population is dedicated to upholding Islamic principles and practices, with prayer times carefully calculated and announced locally to accommodate the congregation's needs.

The desert climate of Abqaiq can be quite extreme, with scorching summers and mild winters. Visitors can best experience the city's attractions and prayer times during the cooler months of November to March. During this period, the community typically comes together for prayer and other religious events, providing a great opportunity for visitors to engage with the local Muslim community and experience Islamic life firsthand.
