Located in the southern part of Dhaka, Bangladesh's capital city, Azimpur is a significant urban area known for its rich cultural heritage and strong community bonds. Home to a diverse population of over a million residents, Azimpur is an integral part of a city where the majority of residents identify as Muslim. It's not uncommon to find mosques and Islamic centers throughout the city, where residents can gather for prayer and community events, emphasizing the importance of Islamic culture in everyday life.

For Muslims living in Azimpur, accessing prayer times is essential to maintaining their faith. With various mosques and Islamic centers available, residents can easily find accurate prayer times, ensuring they never miss a prayer throughout the day. The strong sense of community in Azimpur is fostered through a deep connection to their faith, with prayer being a significant part of daily life. Visitors can experience the resilience and warmth of Bangladeshi culture, where Islam plays a central role in shaping daily routines and community interactions.

Azimpur experiences a tropical savanna climate, characterized by high temperatures and significant rainfall during the monsoon season. When planning a visit, the best time to visit Azimpur is during the cooler months of December and January, when the temperature averages around 20 degrees Celsius. This allows visitors to explore the city comfortably, experience the local culture, and take part in significant events such as Eid celebrations, which bring the community together in a celebration of their faith.
