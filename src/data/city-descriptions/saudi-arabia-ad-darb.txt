Located in the Riyadh Region of Saudi Arabia, Ad Darb is a significant urban center with a population of over 1.8 million residents. This bustling city serves as a key industrial, commercial, and residential hub, playing a vital role in the country's economic growth. As a predominantly Muslim city, Islamic culture and practices heavily influence the way of life, with a strong Muslim community and numerous mosques throughout the area.

The city's predominantly Muslim population ensures that Islamic traditions and customs are deeply ingrained in daily life. Muslims can easily find prayer times and observe their faith in the numerous mosques and Islamic centers that cater to the community's spiritual needs. The city's Islamic atmosphere is evident in the way residents adhere to Islamic practices and values, making it an ideal destination for Muslims seeking to deepen their connection with their faith. The community's strong sense of camaraderie and shared faith creates a unique and welcoming environment for believers from around the world.

The desert climate of Ad Darb means that the best time to visit is during the cooler winter months, from December to February. During this period, the temperature ranges from 12°C to 22°C, making it an ideal time to explore the city's attractions and attend prayer services at various mosques. Travelers can plan their visit accordingly to coincide with the prayer times and experience the city's tranquility during this time.
